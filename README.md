# Hospital Administration Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes hospital admission data to identify patterns and
factors associated with patient readmissions.

The analysis focuses on understanding readmission patterns, patient
characteristics, hospital utilization, length of stay, medical procedures,
medications, and other factors that can support better patient care and
hospital resource planning.

## Business Problem

The hospital experienced increased readmission rates, resulting in higher
healthcare costs and concerns regarding patient outcomes and quality of care.

The objective of this analysis is to identify factors associated with
readmissions and provide data-driven insights that can support targeted
interventions, improve patient outcomes, optimize resource allocation, and
reduce unnecessary costs.

## Dataset

The dataset contains hospital encounter and patient-level information,
including:

- Encounter and patient identifiers
- Demographics such as race, gender, and age
- Length of hospital stay
- Medical specialty
- Laboratory procedures
- Other procedures
- Number of medications
- Previous inpatient, outpatient, and emergency visits
- Diagnosis codes
- Medication indicators
- Diabetes medication information
- Readmission status

The target readmission field indicates whether the patient was readmitted
within 30 days.

## Analysis Performed

### Basic Analysis

- Readmission rates across age groups
- Average hospital stay by medical specialty
- Relationship between emergency visits and readmission rates
- Diabetes-related readmission analysis
- Readmission rates based on diabetic medication changes
- Relationship between laboratory procedures and readmissions
- Readmission rates by race and gender
- Weight category and readmission analysis
- Relationship between medications and hospital stay
- Impact of previous outpatient visits on readmission rates

### Medium-Level Analysis

- Readmission trends over time
- Effect of comorbid conditions on length of stay and readmissions
- Discharge disposition and readmission analysis
- Demographics and medical intervention analysis
- Previous inpatient, outpatient, and emergency visits by diagnosis
- Impact of readmissions on patient outcomes
- Medication prescribing patterns by medical specialty
- Weight categories combined with diagnosis codes
- Diabetic medication effectiveness
- Patient satisfaction and readmission analysis where data is available

### Advanced Analysis

- Predictive modeling for high-risk readmission patients
- Patient segmentation based on healthcare utilization
- Financial impact of readmissions
- Social determinants of health and readmission patterns
- Post-discharge follow-up strategy

## Business Recommendations

- Identify patient groups with higher readmission risk.
- Strengthen post-discharge follow-up for high-risk patients.
- Improve resource allocation based on specialty and patient utilization
  patterns.
- Review medication management strategies for patients at higher risk.
- Monitor patients with frequent emergency, inpatient, or outpatient visits.
- Use readmission insights to support targeted quality-improvement
  initiatives.

## Key Objective

The overall objective is to use data-driven analysis to support improved
patient care, reduce unnecessary readmissions, optimize hospital resources,
and identify opportunities for cost reduction.
